          LEGAL & PRIVACY
          
Privacy Policy & Terms

        

        
      

      

        
1. 100% Local-First Privacy Policy

        

          VDPAI is built from the ground up to respect your digital sovereignty. All conversation histories, system prompts, personal memories, and provider configurations remain stored directly in your Android device's private application storage.
        

        

          No personal data, telemetry, analytics, or behavioral cookies are sent to VDPAI developers or any third-party advertising network.
        


        
2. AI Provider Communications

        

          When you configure an AI API provider (such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, Groq, or DeepSeek), your prompt text and conversation context are transmitted directly over secure HTTPS to that specific provider using your own API key. You are subject to each provider's individual terms and privacy policies.
        


        
3. Backend Server Connectivity

        

          Connecting a remote backend server is completely optional. If connected, authentication uses opaque session grants and CSRF-protected tokens. You may disconnect or revoke sessions at any time.
        


        
4. Terms of Service

        

          VDPAI is provided "as is" without warranties of any kind. You retain full ownership of all prompts and data generated. You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your API keys and server access tokens.
